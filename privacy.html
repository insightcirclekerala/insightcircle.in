Privacy

Privacy, kept simple

Insight Circle is a non-commercial reflective space. We collect as little as we can, and we do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

Entry 01 — The contact form

When you write to us through the contact page, the details you choose to enter (such as your name, email address and message) are passed to FormSubmit, a form-delivery service, and arrive in our email inbox. We use them only to reply to you.

Entry 02 — Measuring visits {% if site.ga4_id != "" %}

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand, in aggregate, how many people visit and which pages they read. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this. We have switched off Google signals and advertising personalisation, and we do not use analytics data to identify individual visitors or to show ads.

You can block these cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's opt-out add-on, without affecting how the site works.

{% else %}

This website does not currently use analytics or tracking cookies.

{% endif %} Entry 03 — Other services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Links to Instagram and WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Entry 04 — Talking to us

For any question about your data, or to ask us to delete a message you sent, write to {{ s.email }}.
