---
title: "Benching"
note: ""
---
Benching refers to keeping someone as a potential romantic partner without actively engaging in a committed relationship. The term is commonly used to describe situations where a person maintains intermittent contact, keeping the connection available as a backup rather than as a primary relationship.

The word comes from team sports, where substitute players wait on the bench until they are called into the game. In relational contexts, benching often reflects uncertainty, avoidance of commitment, or a desire to keep options open without clarity about mutual expectations.
