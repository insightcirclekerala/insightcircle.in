Drawing from professional understanding as well as real-life narratives, the conversation stays close to experiences as they are lived within families, relationships, and caregiving contexts. Rather than offering prescriptions, the session invites shared reflection on distress, support, and care, and how family well-being is shaped during these early, vulnerable phases of life.
